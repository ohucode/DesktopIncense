# Privacy Policy for Desktop Incense

**Effective date: October 29, 2026**

Desktop Incense is designed to work without collecting your personal information. This policy explains the app's privacy practices and how information you choose to share through support is handled.

## Information collected by the app

Desktop Incense does not collect or transmit personal information, usage data, or device identifiers to the developer. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics services.

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal information collected through the app for the developer to retain, sell, or share.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app stores preferences and app state locally on your Mac so it can remember settings such as incense visibility, smoke size, the selected display, the holder's position, and accumulated ash. This information is used only to provide the app's functionality and is not transmitted to the developer.

You can change preferences through the app's menu bar controls and remove accumulated ash using **Clean Ash**.

## Support requests

If you choose to contact us through GitHub, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your issue or message, such as a description of a problem, your app or macOS version, and any screenshots you attach. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the reported problem.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include sensitive personal information, and check screenshots before posting. GitHub operates separately from Desktop Incense and handles information under its own privacy policy.

## Apple services

Apple may process information when you download the app or use App Store services. Those services are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Apple account and device settings. This policy describes Desktop Incense's practices, not Apple's independent services.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date to reflect those changes.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please [open an issue in the Desktop Incense support repository](https://github.com/ohucode/DesktopIncense/issues/new). A GitHub account is required to post. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
